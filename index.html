How it works

1

List your skills

Add what you can teach and what you want to learn. Guitar, graphic design, cooking, video editing: anything counts.

2

Get matched

We suggest people nearby who have the skill you need and want the one you offer.

3

Swap and rate

Meet up or connect online, trade skills, then rate each other so the next person knows what to expect.

Know who you're swapping with.

After every swap, both people leave a rating. Each profile shows a reputation score, so you can decide before you say yes.
